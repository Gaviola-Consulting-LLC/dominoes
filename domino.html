Rules

  • Double-six is the highest double.
  • When you cannot move, the game draws from the boneyard until you can play.
  • There is only one spinner, and both players play from that same spinner.
  • Spinner arms open horizontal first (left and right), then vertical (up and down).
  • Doubles count as double when scoring exposed ends.
  • After both left and right spinner arms are occupied, the center spinner no longer counts for points.
  • A tile is legal only when at least one pip matches the selected open side.
  • Tiles played on spinner up/down are vertical unless the tile is a double.
  • After each play, score only the exposed outside ends whenever their total is a multiple of 5.
  • If the board is locked because neither player can move and the boneyard is empty, the player with fewer hand pips wins the opponent's exact hand pips.
  • If a player goes out, they score the opponent's exact remaining hand pips.
  • First to 250 wins the match.
